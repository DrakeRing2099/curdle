Dairy Twin · System View

one simulated year, replayed day by day from the Python twin

What you are looking at. Each box is a place that holds dairy stock. Bars inside a box show stock per product against that place's capacity; the darker part of a bar is stock made in the last two days. Moving dashed lines are today's flows, with width and speed set by the number of units; the label is the count. Red numbers under a box are units thrown away there today.

The kirana city. The grid is the twelve localities, each on its own power feeder. Dots are the 240 kirana shops, coloured by the measure chosen above; shops with a black ring have a battery-backed brand fridge. A locality turns violet while its power is cut, with the hours shown.

Customers. Households per channel against where they started the year; kirana customers who find an empty shelf may move to the delivery apps (the “spill” arrow) and drift back when kiranas stay stocked.

The plan. Every Monday the policy reads the twin and chooses the week's plan. For the learned policy the situation it saw (its compressed MDP state) is shown too.

Timeline. Red bands are heat waves, gold ticks festival weeks, the violet strip at the bottom is how many localities lost power that day. Click or drag to scrub. Other loaded runs appear as faint lines for comparison. Keys: space play/pause, ← → one day, ⇧+arrows one week.

Loading your own run. Export any policy, scenario and seed with python experiments/export_run.py --policy Learned --scenario severe --seed 7 and drop the JSON file on this page. Change the twin's code or parameters, re-export, and this page shows the new behaviour.
