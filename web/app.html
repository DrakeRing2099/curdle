Dairy Twin Control Room

A stochastic digital twin of one dairy brand's city network: one plant, 240 kiranas run as actors, 4 supermarkets behind a brand warehouse, and 6 quick-commerce dark stores filled straight from the plant. Every day is a fresh random draw. Pick a weekly policy, including one the twin learned by itself, then trigger a heat wave or a power cut and watch it spread.

    Median over simulated years, with the 5th–95th percentile range underneath. Reward = annual profit − ₹10 × lost units, in lakh, the score the learned policy was trained on. Goal met = share of weeks the goal predicate holds: rolling 4-week fill at or above 92% (kirana), 95% (modern trade), 92% (quick-commerce) for every product, waste under its cap, and cash above zero. Pattern goals = share of years in which all three behaviour goals hold: back to the kirana target within 2 weeks of every heat wave, no channel below 85% for more than 2 weeks running, and no sector carrying more than 20% of kirana waste. All data is synthetic.
